About

Planner, GIS Analyst, Storyteller

City & Regional Planning senior at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, graduating in May 2027.

I design places, then use GIS to test them against the land: where housing fits, where water goes, and where risk sits. At the Port of Tacoma I turned 40 years of field records into a single map of a former landfill. Now I'm a Student Researcher at Cal Poly's WUI FIRE Institute.

Two seasons as a wildland firefighter shaped how I read a landscape. Outside planning I captain Cal Poly's club Ultimate team.

You have a remarkable ability to do the research, understand the complexities, and then communicate your findings confidently, clearly, and visually in a way that is both compelling and accessible.

One of the greatest challenges I've seen planners face throughout my jobs is translating their tremendous technical expertise and deep knowledge into a story that resonates with others... What may surprise you is that you already possess this skill at a level that exceeds many of your peers, and even some professionals who have spent decades in the field.

Alice McDaniel · Government Affairs, Port of Tacoma · Letter to Oliver, August 2026
