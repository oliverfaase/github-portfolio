Project Fact Sheet

This fact sheet documents the interpreted former landfill footprint, Port parcels, measured subsurface cross sections, environmental investigation records, contaminant screening, habitat areas, and the methane-monitoring network.

The project integrates historical environmental records with an automated Python and ArcPy workflow designed to improve data traceability, subsurface visualization, QA/QC, and technical communication.
