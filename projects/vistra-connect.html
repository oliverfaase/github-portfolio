Vistra Connect Specific Plan

Morro Bay's decommissioned power plant site reimagined as a mixed-use waterfront district.

Overview

Turning Morro Bay's decommissioned power plant site into a mixed-use waterfront district that links downtown, the Embarcadero, and the neighborhoods north of Morro Rock. Our seven-person team kept development out of sensitive habitat with setbacks and buffers, and restored wetlands and dunes along Morro Creek.
