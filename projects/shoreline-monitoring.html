Professional Project • Port of Tacoma

Shoreline Monitoring Experience Builder

Repeatable drone surveys organized for year-over-year comparison and GeoAI change detection.

Overview

I developed an ArcGIS Experience Builder application supporting long-term shoreline monitoring throughout the Port of Tacoma. Predefined drone flight locations and automated flight plans collect imagery from consistent positions, supporting comparison of vegetation change, sediment, erosion, and bank stability.
