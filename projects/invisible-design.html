Study Abroad • DIS Copenhagen, Fall 2025

Invisible Design

Field sketches and photos on why Copenhagen feels so easy to live in.

Overview

Why does Copenhagen feel so easy to live in? A semester of field sketches and photos found the answer in small, human-centered design choices.

Water
Squares like Tåsinge Plads store stormwater in plain sight
Bikes
Stair rails, footrests at lights, and room on trains
Access
Step-free metro and benches marking future water levels

Poster

Takeaways

Climate adaptation works best when it doubles as everyday public space.

  • Many U.S. cities treat stormwater, bike lanes, and accessibility as separate engineering checklists. Copenhagen shows they can be designed together as public space people enjoy.
  • Pair green infrastructure with plazas and play spaces so it gets funded and cared for.
  • Small, low-cost details like bike rails, footrests, and step-free entries can ride along with routine street projects.
  • Design can teach: benches that mark future water levels make climate risk visible.
