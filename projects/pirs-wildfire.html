PIRS for Wildfire

Mapping how a community's plans affect wildfire risk, district by district.

Overview

Wildfire guidance usually sits in a few documents, while the plans that guide growth may never mention fire. PIRS reviews all of a community's plans together and maps how each policy is likely to affect wildfire risk, district by district. The February 2026 Guidebook comes from Cal Poly's WUI FIRE Institute and Texas A&M's Hazard Reduction & Recovery Center.
