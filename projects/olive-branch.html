Academic Project • Olive St and Santa Rosa, San Luis Obispo

The Olive Branch

A car-oriented intersection redesigned with courtyard homes, bike lanes, and a public plaza.

Overview

The Olive Street and Santa Rosa intersection links downtown, Highway 101, and nearby neighborhoods, but it was built for cars passing through. Guided by three goals (reduce traffic and noise, make the place desirable, increase density), the plan adds 45 courtyard homes, bike lanes, a transit stop, a food truck court, and a playground.
