Professional Project • Port of Tacoma, Summer 2026

Bathymetric Surfaces

Hydrographic surveys turned into gap-checked depth rasters clipped to the shoreline.

Overview

The Port's waterways are dredged, navigated, and monitored, so their depth has to be precise. I processed hydrographic survey data and converted TIN surfaces into rasters clipped to the curved shoreline and waterway edges, then checked them for gaps.

Maps

What It Showed and Solves

Uses
Dredging, navigation, environmental monitoring, port planning
Tools
ArcGIS Pro, TIN to raster, terrain modeling, QA/QC
Showed
A deep channel below -90 ft and a shallow bench near -20 ft, mapped as continuous surfaces instead of scattered soundings.
Solves
One gap-checked surface that follows the real shoreline, so dredging volumes and navigation clearances aren't skewed by data outside the water.
Next
Future surveys can be compared against this baseline to track sediment buildup and plan maintenance dredging.
