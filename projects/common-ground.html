Common Ground

A 17-acre former industrial site in San Luis Obispo redesigned as a 230-home neighborhood.

Overview

Common Ground turns a 17-acre former industrial site at 1150 Laurel Lane in San Luis Obispo into a neighborhood: 230 homes across apartments, townhomes, duplexes, and mixed-use buildings, with corner retail and a central community center.

The plan is organized around movement. Walking and bike routes link green courtyards to nearby streets and the Railroad Safety Trail, while cars stay on a traffic-calmed loop. Bioswales, tree canopy, and landscaped buffers handle stormwater and shade.
