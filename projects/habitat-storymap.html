Habitat Assessment StoryMap

Port habitat restoration sites turned into a public map tour with drone imagery.

Explore StoryMap

Overview

I developed an interactive ArcGIS StoryMap showcasing habitat assessment and restoration sites throughout the Port of Tacoma. It combines interactive mapping, high-resolution drone imagery, site descriptions, and aerial flyover videos, so visitors can explore conditions and restoration work site by site.
